Guitar Dashboard

An interactive music theory dashboard, including a circle of fifths for guitarists.

Discover how scales and modes map to the fretboard and how chords work within scales.

Tips: tap a key or a mode to change the scale. Tap a chord on the circle of fifths to highlight it, or tap notes on the fretboard to hear and mark them. Use the gear on the fretboard for tuning and left-handed options. The speaker icon switches sound on and off.

Works fully offline. Based on the open source Guitar Dashboard by Mike Hadlow (MIT licence).
